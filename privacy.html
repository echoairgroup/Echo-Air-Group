Privacy Policy

How Echo Air Group collects, uses, stores and protects personal data.

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Data controller

Echo Air Group is a private, non-commercial virtual airline and flight-simulation hobby project operated by an individual in the Netherlands. Echo Air Group is not a separate registered company, partnership or association.

Privacy contact: echoairgrouptiktok@gmail.com

Website: https://echoairgroup.io/

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies when you visit echoairgroup.io, create or use an Echo Air Group account, submit an application or contact form, use the virtual airline's pilot features, or interact with integrations provided through the website.

It explains the personal data that may be processed, the reasons and legal bases for processing, the service providers involved, retention periods, and the rights available to you under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Dutch law.

Echo Air Group is a virtual airline for flight-simulation enthusiasts. It is not a real-world airline, aircraft operator, aviation authority or provider of real-world flight training.

2. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is the individual operating the Echo Air Group website and virtual airline under the name Echo Air Group. There is no separate legal entity behind this hobby project.

For all data-protection questions, access requests, correction or deletion requests, objections, or complaints, contact echoairgrouptiktok@gmail.com. Please do not include your password in an email.

No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed. If a DPO or representative becomes legally required, this policy will be updated with the relevant contact information.

The information processed depends on which features you choose to use. Echo Air Group does not need every category of data for every visitor.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, personal data is processed only where a legal basis under Article 6 GDPR is available. Depending on the feature, the relevant basis is:

  • Providing requested account and pilot features — Article 6(1)(b): creating and maintaining an account, signing you in, matching flight data to your profile, showing statistics and providing features you request.
  • Responding to applications and enquiries — Article 6(1)(b) and/or Article 6(1)(f): taking steps at your request, handling correspondence and operating the voluntary virtual-airline community. Where legitimate interests are relied on, the interests are limited to administering the project, preventing misuse and responding to requests, and are balanced against your rights.
  • Security and service operation — Article 6(1)(f): protecting accounts and infrastructure, diagnosing errors, preventing abuse and maintaining service reliability.
  • Cookie preference records — Article 6(1)(f), and Article 6(1)(c) where applicable: remembering and administering your choices, and retaining evidence where needed to demonstrate compliance with applicable consent obligations.
  • Optional cookies or similar technologies — consent where required: optional analytics or marketing technologies will not be activated unless the relevant consent has been given. No optional analytics or advertising trackers are currently integrated into the website.
  • Legal obligations — Article 6(1)(c): responding to binding legal requirements where these apply.

5. NewSky integration

If you add a NewSky Pilot ID to your Echo Air Group account, the website may use that identifier to retrieve or synchronise flight information associated with your NewSky profile. The imported information can include flight identifiers, airports, aircraft, flight times, duration, distance and ratings, depending on the data made available by NewSky.

This information is used for your pilot profile, flight history, statistics, rankings, achievements and related virtual-airline features. NewSky is a separate service and its own privacy terms apply to the information it processes on its platform.

6. Discord integration

Connecting Discord is optional. If you connect your Discord account or use a feature that links it, the website may process your Discord user ID and the link status. The ID may be used for role synchronisation where that feature is configured.

Echo Air Group does not require or request your Discord password. Discord processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Applications and contact forms

Pilot and staff applications are submitted through Forminit and may also be copied to the private Echo Air Group application-management area backed by the website database. The information may include your contact details and the answers you choose to submit. Administrators may add a recruitment status or internal note to manage the application.

The contact form currently uses FormSubmit to deliver messages to the Echo Air Group contact inbox. Forminit and FormSubmit are separate services and may process submitted data to provide their form-delivery services.

Please provide only information that is relevant to your application or enquiry. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identification numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Cookies and browser storage

The website currently does not intentionally set advertising, behavioural-tracking or analytics cookies. The website does use essential browser storage, including local storage and session storage, for account sign-in, administrator sessions, booking confirmation details and cookie preferences. Browser storage is not always technically a cookie, but it can still store or access information on your device and is described in the Cookie Policy.

Optional analytics and marketing categories are off unless you actively choose otherwise. No optional analytics or advertising trackers are currently integrated. You can review or change your choice at any time using Cookie Settings in the website footer.

When you save a cookie preference, the website creates a random browser reference. If you are signed in at that time, the current preference may be associated with your Echo account. The consent record does not include your IP address or a device fingerprint.

9. Service providers and recipients

Personal data is made available only where needed to operate the website, provide a feature you request, or meet a legal obligation. Depending on the feature, recipients may include:

  • Render: hosting and operation of the Echo Air Group backend.
  • Neon: hosting the PostgreSQL database used for account, flight, application, platform and cookie-preference records.
  • NewSky: an external source of pilot and flight information for the synchronisation features you use.
  • Discord: account-linking and role-synchronisation features, where enabled.
  • Forminit: delivery of pilot and staff application forms.
  • FormSubmit: delivery of contact-form messages to the Echo Air Group inbox.
  • External asset providers: where a page loads fonts, images or other resources from external domains (for example Google Fonts, Discord-hosted media or TikTok-hosted assets), those providers receive the normal technical information required for the browser request.

Echo Air Group does not sell personal data and does not use the website to run targeted advertising. Service providers may process data under their own terms and, where applicable, on behalf of Echo Air Group.

10. International transfers

Some service providers or their infrastructure may process information outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where a transfer is subject to the GDPR, the relevant provider must rely on an applicable transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards under the GDPR. You may contact us to ask which provider is relevant to a particular feature and how to obtain further information about applicable safeguards.

11. How long data is kept

Personal data is kept only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal obligations and the retention periods below. These periods are review targets; data held in provider backups or operational logs may follow the provider's own deletion and backup cycle.

12. Your rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal data concerning you and receive a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of personal data.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive certain data in a portable format where the right to portability applies.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.
  • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, where that decision produces legal or similarly significant effects, subject to applicable exceptions.

Echo Air Group calculates some flight statistics and rankings automatically from flight records. These calculations are used for virtual-airline features and are not intended to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

13. How to make a privacy request

Send your request to echoairgrouptiktok@gmail.com. Explain which right you wish to exercise and provide enough information for us to locate the relevant account or data. We may ask for reasonable verification before disclosing or changing personal data, to prevent unauthorised access.

We will respond within the time limits set by the GDPR, normally within one month. Where a request is complex or numerous, the period may be extended where the GDPR permits; you will be informed of the extension and the reasons.

14. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect the website and its data, including password hashing, authenticated access to administrative functions and hosted database infrastructure. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials private and for using a secure, unique password. Contact us promptly if you believe your Echo Air Group account has been accessed without permission.

15. Children

The website is a hobby community for flight-simulation enthusiasts and is not specifically designed for children. If you are under the age at which you may independently provide consent under applicable law, obtain the involvement of a parent or guardian before submitting personal information where required.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so that we can review the situation.

16. Complaints

You may contact Echo Air Group first so that we can try to resolve a concern. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens: autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

17. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, integrations, data-processing activities or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page. If a change requires renewed consent for optional technologies, the cookie preference interface will ask you again.
