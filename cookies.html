Cookie Policy

How Echo Air Group uses cookies and similar browser-storage technologies.

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0

At a glance

Echo Air Group does not currently intentionally set advertising, behavioural-tracking or analytics cookies. The website does use essential browser storage for account access, administrator sessions, booking confirmation details and remembering your cookie preference.

You can change your choice at any time using Cookie Settings in the website footer.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored by a website in your browser. Websites can also use similar technologies, including local storage and session storage, to keep information on your device or read information already stored there.

Although local storage and session storage are not technically HTTP cookies, they can involve storing or accessing information on your device. This policy explains both cookies and the browser-storage mechanisms currently used by Echo Air Group.

2. What does Echo Air Group currently use?

The current website code does not intentionally create conventional HTTP cookies or use an analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking cookie service. It uses the first-party browser storage entries listed below. This inventory reflects the website code at the date shown above and may change when features or integrations are updated.

3. Cookie categories and your choices

Essential browser storage

Essential storage supports functions you request, such as account sign-in, administrator access, the booking confirmation feature and remembering your cookie choice. The website needs this storage for those functions. It is always enabled and is not presented as an optional category.

Analytics

Analytics technologies can measure how a website is used to help improve its performance. Echo Air Group does not currently have an analytics cookie or analytics tracker integrated into the website. This category is off unless you actively choose to allow it.

Advertising and marketing

Advertising or marketing technologies can be used to measure campaigns or personalise advertising. Echo Air Group does not currently use advertising or marketing cookies or trackers. This category is off unless you actively choose to allow it.

Choosing “Accept all” records permission for the optional categories shown in the preference interface. Choosing “Reject optional” records that those categories are not allowed. “Customise” lets you select the optional categories individually. No optional tracking service is activated by the current website, even if you choose to accept all.

4. How to change or withdraw your choice

You can open Cookie Settings from the footer on any page. From there, you can review the categories, change your selection and save the new preference. You do not need to contact Echo Air Group to change your cookie choice.

Changing your choice updates the preference saved for that browser. If you clear local storage or use another browser/device, the website may ask you again because it cannot read the preference stored in the original browser.

5. Cookie preference records in the admin panel

When you accept, reject or customise your preference, the website sends the latest choice to the Echo Air Group backend. The record contains a random browser reference, the selected categories, the policy version and the time of the latest update. If you are signed in at the moment you save your choice, your Echo user account may also be associated with that record. If you are not signed in, the record is displayed to authorised administrators as an anonymous browser reference.

The consent record does not include your IP address or a device fingerprint. The admin panel displays the latest preference per browser reference, rather than a history of every click. Inactive server-side preference records are removed after 24 months without an update.

6. Third-party content

Some pages may request fonts, images or other files from external services, such as Google Fonts, Discord-hosted media or TikTok-hosted assets. When your browser requests an asset from an external domain, that provider receives the technical information normally required to deliver the request, such as your IP address and request metadata. These requests are separate from first-party Echo Air Group cookies and are subject to the relevant provider's privacy and cookie practices.

Echo Air Group does not control cookies or browser storage that a third-party provider may set on its own domain. The providers and assets used by the website may change as the website is updated.

7. Managing browser storage yourself

You can view, block or delete cookies and site storage through your browser's privacy or site-settings controls. The exact steps depend on your browser. Clearing local storage or session storage may sign you out, remove booking confirmation details and reset your cookie preference. The website may then display the cookie banner again.

Blocking all storage may affect account sign-in, administrator access, booking confirmations or other features that rely on browser storage.

8. Personal data and your privacy rights

Some browser-storage entries contain or relate to personal data, such as an account token or passenger name in a booking confirmation. Information about how Echo Air Group processes personal data, the legal bases used, retention periods, your rights and how to contact the operator is set out in the Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to this Cookie Policy

This policy will be updated if Echo Air Group introduces or removes cookies, browser-storage entries, analytics, advertising tools or other relevant technologies. If a change requires a new choice, the preference interface will ask you again.
